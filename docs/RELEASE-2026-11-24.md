## Page layout and style

### Desktop layout

- The page uses a light blue-gray page background: `#f4f8fc`.
- The hero section uses a blue gradient from `#203a61` to `#4A90E2`, with white text.
- The hero heading is 2.8rem and has a top/bottom margin of 8px.
- The “Student Notice” label is gold (`#f6cf65`), bold, 1.05rem, with spaced lettering.
- Desktop content uses a two-column grid with 28px gaps and a maximum width of 950px.
- Test cards are white, have an 18px radius, 30px padding, a 6px blue top border, and a soft blue-gray shadow.
- Date panels use `#edf5ff` with blue text (`#205080`), 12px radius, and 12px/16px padding.
- Chapter rows are light blue-gray (`#f8fbff`), outlined with `#e3edf8`, and use 10px radius and 13px padding.
- Chapter numbers use 34px circular blue badges with white text.
- The reminder banner uses a pale yellow background (`#fff3cd`), dark gold text (`#6f5600`), 12px radius, and bold text.

### Mobile layout

- On screens 700px wide or smaller, the hero padding is compact: 18px horizontally and 14px vertically.
- The hero heading is 1.45rem with 1.2 line height.
- The Student Notice label is 0.75rem with a small bottom margin.
- Both test cards remain side-by-side in a two-column grid on mobile rather than stacking vertically.
- Mobile grid gap is 8px.
- Content is limited to 20px less than the viewport width and has 12px top/18px bottom padding.
- Cards use 10px radius, 9px/7px padding, and a 4px blue top border.
- Date panels use 7px radius and 7px/6px padding; icons are 0.82rem, day labels 0.78rem, and dates 0.66rem.
- Card headings are 0.86rem with compact margins.
- Test descriptions are 0.68rem with 1.15 line height.
- Chapter rows use 5px gaps, 6px radius, 5px/4px padding, and 0.68rem text.
- Chapter badges are 20px circles with 0.68rem text.
- The reminder banner uses 8px top margin, 7px radius, 7px/8px padding, 0.72rem text, and 1.25 line height.
- Portrait mobile mode further reduces the hero and content vertical padding.
